The bay

your bike knows before you do

A bike tells you everything. A half-second hesitation off the line. A chain that sings one note flat. Brake lever that travels a finger too far. You already felt it — you just didn't have anyone to hand it to.

That's the whole reason this place exists. On R.A. Khan Chowdhury Road, we open the bay, put your machine on the stand, and go through it the slow way. Not the fast way. The slow way is the only way that holds.

The process

      
five steps, nothing skipped

    


    

      

        Step 01
        
You call, message, or roll in. We listen to the bike and to you — the symptom matters as much as the reading.

        
intake

      

      

        Step 02
        
On the stand, front to back. We show you what we found before we touch a bolt. No invisible work.

        
diagnosis

      

      

        Step 03
        
A written number, agreed before we start. Parts listed by name and brand, so you know exactly what's going in.

        
the quote

      

      

        Step 04
        
Genuine parts, correct torque, clean bay. The old parts come back to you in a bag if you want them.

        
the work

      

      

        Step 05
        
Road test, wash, handover. Then we check in after a week — because a service isn't done at the gate.
