    07
    
  
    Straight answers
    
  
    questions riders actually ask
    

  
    
    
    
      How much does a bike service cost in Kushtia?
      
    It depends on the machine and what it needs — anyone who quotes you before seeing the bike is guessing. We inspect it first, then give you one written price before any work starts. That price is what you pay. If something changes mid-job we call you before touching it, never after.
    
    
    
    
    
      Are your parts genuine?
      
    Yes. Every part we fit arrives sealed and in its box, and you're welcome to ask to see the box before it goes on the bike. We also hand back your old parts on request, so you can see exactly what was replaced and why.
    
    
    
    
    
      How long will my bike be with you?
      
    Most standard services are same-day. A wash is under an hour. Paint and bigger modification work gets a completion date agreed up front, so you can plan around it instead of waiting on a phone call.
    
    
    
    
    
      Do I have to pay a deposit to book?
      
    No. Booking costs nothing and we hold the slot on your word. You pay after the work is done and you've seen it. No card, no advance, no catch.
    
    
    
    
    
      Which bikes do you work on?
      
    All the common lineups on Bangladeshi roads — Yamaha, Honda, Bajaj, Suzuki, TVS, Hero, Runner, Lifan — plus imported machines. We service cars as well, which is why the sign says 360°.
    
    
    
    
    
      What if something goes wrong after the service?
      
    Bring it straight back. If it's something we touched, we fix it and we don't argue about it. We also check in about a week after a service to make sure it's still riding the way it should.
    
    
    
    
    
      Can I get parts without booking a service?
      
    Of course. Reserve anything from the shop and collect it at the counter, or have us fit it in the bay while you wait. Don't see your model listed? Call us — most parts are in the back or here within a couple of days.
