# Moto Market control room — staff guide

The quickest way to learn the control room is the guided tour. Tap the mascot **?** in the top bar, the sidebar card "First time here? Take a 1-minute tour" or "Open the guided tour" to open the tour hub. Pick a chapter and Cookie Rider points at each part of the screen. Nothing is saved or published while the tour runs. Every page also has an "Explain this page" button, and search (press /) finds tour chapters too, for example "add subcategory". Reset your tour progress under Store settings → Your sign-in.

## Save, preview, publish

Open the control room and sign in with the details your Administrator gives you. Home has shortcuts for common jobs.

1. Choose what to change.
2. Make the edit and Save as draft.
3. Preview the actual website, including phone view.
4. Review & publish. Open storefronts update automatically.

Drafts stay private. Every save shows a message with **Undo** for a few seconds, which puts the draft back the way it was. If another staff member saves first, reload the latest draft before saving yours. Administrators can undo a publication in Website → Version history.

Delete and Duplicate sit at the bottom of every editor and in each row's "…" menu. Before a delete, the control room lists everything that uses the item and, where needed, asks where to move it (for example the products of a category). If you close an editor with unsaved changes, it asks before discarding them.

## Products, models and colours

Products → Edit controls the name, price, discount, description, availability, category and pictures. Leave discount price empty when there is no offer. The jump bar at the top of the product editor goes straight to Price & stock, Photos, Colours, Fits, Data sheet, Advanced (SKU, tags) and Visibility. Add up to 12 extra photos at once; drag to reorder, Make main, or add alt text. "Save & add another" opens a fresh form in the same category.

Any category, subcategory, brand, brand group, page or service category can be created on the spot: pick "+ New …" at the bottom of its list. It is saved together with the form.

Products → Categories lists each category with its subcategory and product counts. Subcategories live inside the category editor: rename them in place, add a photo, hide them, drag them into order (the order is the website order) or delete them (their products move to the subcategory you choose). A new category can also add its menu link, homepage card and shop carousel for you.

The All products list has search (name, brand, SKU, tags), filters, sorting and "Show more". Tick rows (or long-press on a phone) to move, hide, reprice, put on sale, restock, rebrand, add to a bundle or section, duplicate or delete many products at once. Export (CSV) downloads every product; Import from spreadsheet shows a preview before anything changes. Home shows the products running low.

Choose Selected vehicles and tick every compatible bike or car model. A Universal product must specify Bikes, Cars or both. Keep Ask us to confirm if compatibility is uncertain. Customers search a model directly and see the products assigned to it. Add models under Products → Vehicles.

Available colours → Add colour lets you enter the actual name, swatch, availability and optional picture. Customers choose a colour before adding the part. Orders show their exact selection.

## Photos, banners and website content

Upload JPG, PNG or WebP pictures in Photos, or use an item's Upload button. Choose photo reuses an existing picture. Banner controls include a phone picture, focus point, destination and order.

Website → Layout shows the homepage and shop from top to bottom. Drag or use the arrows to move a part, switch it off to hide it, and use + Add section for product carousels and grids (a category, subcategory, brand, deals, new arrivals or hand-picked products), category tiles, bundles, brand strips, promo banners, FAQs and text. Preview draft shows the result before publishing. Products → Brands manages brand logos and the brand groups shown on the homepage. Photos has search, filters, multi-file upload and a details sheet with where-used, name, alt text, Replace everywhere and Delete (only when unused).

Website → Page content groups editable text, pictures and links by section. Website menu controls links, labels, destinations and order. Product and service subcategories appear as dropdowns. Brand logos and Store settings control identity and contact details.

Services controls categories, pictures, descriptions, included work, fixed prices or inspection quotes, duration and booking availability. The homepage service/shop blocks have their own icons and destinations.

## Pages & Posts

Create page accepts HTML and CSS. The theme supplies fonts, colours, header and footer. Scripts and forms are disabled.

Create post accepts a title, address, cover picture, date, author, introduction and body. Use blank lines for paragraphs, ## for a heading and - for a bullet. The theme supplies the layout. After publishing, add a menu link if wanted.

## Orders & bookings

Requests arrive automatically. Open one, contact the customer, update its status and save private notes. A receipt confirms that the request was received; staff still confirm stock, payment and time.

## Team and passwords

Administrators use Store settings → Manage staff to add staff, change usernames/roles, allow publishing, reset passwords or disable access.

- Administrator: all content, publishing, requests and staff access.
- Content Manager: content, products and pictures; publishing when allowed.
- Service Coordinator: orders and bookings.

Everyone can change their own password under Your sign-in. Only Administrators can change another person's credentials. Use at least 14 characters and keep passwords private.
